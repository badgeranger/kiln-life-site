Before booking the diagnostic

A small, defined first step.

The diagnostic is for one recurring report, visibility problem or owner decision. It is not a technical audit of an entire company.

Check the current manual reporting cost →
Good questions

“Can I accept more work next month?” “Why does cash feel tight when revenue is growing?” “Which leads deserve follow-up?”

Before the session

Complete the enquiry form and, if safe, describe the current report. Do not send passwords, customer records or confidential exports.

During 60 minutes

Clarify the decision, useful numbers, current tools, manual steps, data gaps and the smallest dashboard worth building.

Within two working days

Receive a plain-language problem summary, number and source map, dashboard sketch and fixed-scope recommendation.

Not included

Full data cleaning, production integrations, implementation or unlimited follow-up. Those are quoted separately if needed.

Scheduling

A time is agreed by email. Rescheduling or cancellation terms are confirmed before payment or the session is booked.

Clear boundaries

You own the dashboard. The scope stays visible.

✓ Software and paid connector costs are agreed separately.

✓ Data cleaning beyond the agreed sources is re-scoped before work begins.

✓ AI synthesis is separated from calculated metrics and requires human review.

✓ No unlimited revisions, hidden subscriptions or invented performance claims.
