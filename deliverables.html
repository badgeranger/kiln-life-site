What you receive

A dashboard you can use after the project ends.

The result is not only a presentation. You receive a working view of the business, clear definitions for the numbers and the information needed to operate or extend it.

In plain language

You do not need to choose the technology. The exact pack depends on the size of the project, but every item must help you understand the business or maintain the dashboard.

Open the prototype
01Business question

A short statement of what the dashboard should help you understand or decide. Example: “Can we accept more work next month without harming delivery?”

02Number definitions

A simple list explaining each important number, how it is calculated and where it comes from.

03Data-source map

The agreed spreadsheets, accounting tools, project systems or other sources used by the dashboard—plus any gaps that still require manual input.

04Working business dashboard

The actual owner view, tested with the agreed information and organised around money, demand, delivery and the next action.

05Weekly briefing

A short summary of what changed, what may need attention and which questions should be checked. Any AI-written text is separated from calculated facts and reviewed by a person.

06Setup and operating guide

How the information refreshes, what still needs manual input, who owns each source and what to check when a number looks wrong.

07Training and handover

A walkthrough of the dashboard and the agreed support period so you are not dependent on unexplained technical work.

What changes by package

The scope grows only when the business needs it.

A

Dashboard Diagnostic

Problem summary · number list · source check · dashboard sketch · fixed-scope recommendation.

B

Solo Business Dashboard

One owner view · up to eight key numbers · one or two sources · weekly briefing · training.

C

Small Business Dashboard

Owner, sales and delivery views · up to four sources · automated refresh where supported · documentation and support.

D

Advanced Dashboard & Automation

Several systems · forecasts or scenarios · repeated workflow automation · optional source-linked AI summaries · production handover.

The public prototype uses synthetic business data and transparent browser-based rules. It demonstrates the structure of a deliverable; it does not contain client information and is not presented as a live autonomous AI system.
