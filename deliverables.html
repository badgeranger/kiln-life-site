What you receive

Outputs built to survive the meeting.

Every engagement ends with a decision record and usable working assets—not only presentation slides. The exact pack is fixed in the scope before work begins.

A working pack

The artifacts below are examples. A diagnostic uses a compact subset; a sprint combines the pieces needed for one decision.

Operate the sample
01Diagnostic note

One-page statement of the question, current evidence, central risk, recommendation and three next actions. Delivered as a concise PDF or shared document.

02Decision charter

The decision to make, who owns it, the criteria, constraints, stakeholders and evidence still required.

03Research and evidence synthesis

Patterns from interviews, product data, market evidence and existing work—separated from assumptions and interpretation.

04Option and business-case model

Explicit assumptions, value drivers, costs, dependencies and scenario comparisons so the recommendation can be challenged.

05Decision and data map

The business decision, required signals, source systems, ownership, freshness and gaps. Designed before requesting broad data access.

06Interactive prototype

A testable product, dashboard, workflow or operating model used to validate direction before production investment.

07AI workflow specification

Inputs, model or tool boundary, prompt/workflow logic, evaluation approach, human review, fallback and escalation.

08Risk and readiness register

Data, operational, user, technical and governance risks with owners and mitigation—not a generic compliance checklist.

0990-day action plan

Sequenced experiments, delivery work, owners, measures and decision gates for the next operating period.

10Executive decision brief

The recommendation, why now, evidence, options rejected, implications and the decision required from leadership.
