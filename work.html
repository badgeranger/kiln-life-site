Relevant background

Experience turning complicated information into usable decisions.

Kiln combines commercial reporting, digital product delivery and applied AI. The examples below show the methods, outputs and results most relevant to building useful business dashboards.

The working range

Commercial measurement

SQL analysisKPI definitionsTableau reporting

Make business reporting reliable enough to act on.

Regional and platform reports made performance difficult to compare and could hide double-counting behind strong top-line numbers.

What was built

Clear metric definitions, SQL-based analysis, comparison rules and Tableau views that separated real performance from reporting noise.

Why it matters here

A business dashboard is useful only when every number has a clear meaning and can be traced back to an agreed source.

Outcome

Inflated reporting was identified and commercial decisions could be made from a more consistent evidence base.

Complex digital delivery

Five platformsFive regionsCross-functional launch

Turn many moving parts into one coordinated operating view.

A global digital service had to meet different platform, market and operational requirements without losing a shared launch window.

What was built

A requirements map, dependency plan, readiness criteria, review rhythm and escalation structure.

Why it matters here

Small-business dashboards also need to connect sales, delivery and ownership rather than displaying isolated charts.

Outcome

The release cleared all five target platforms within one coordinated launch window.

Applied AI product

LLM workflowInteraction designHuman review

Use AI as a reviewable assistant—not as unexplained magic.

An LLM-based product needed a defined user need, controlled interaction, quality checks and a practical release path, not only a prompt.

What was built

Use-case framing, conversational flow, prompt and workflow iteration, evaluation checks and launch coordination.

Why it matters here

When AI is useful in a dashboard, its job is to explain changes or draft a briefing while the underlying numbers and human decision stay visible.

Outcome

The concept was taken through to a usable product workflow and launch.
